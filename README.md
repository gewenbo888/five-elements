# The Five Elements · 五行人格

A bilingual (EN / 中文) Wu Xing personality assessment. Answer 28 forced-choice scenarios and discover your **elemental composition** across the five archetypes — and read the generation & control cycles that shape your growth.

| 金 Metal · **Blade** | 木 Wood · **Awakening** | 水 Water · **Flow** | 火 Fire · **Wind** | 土 Earth · **Soul** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cutting — sharpness & rules | Growth — understanding | Control — riding the trend | Ignition — rebellion | Bearing — stability |

The classification engine scores each answer toward one element, normalizes to a composition summing to 100%, ranks primary + secondary, and derives cycle-based growth advice (相生 generation = your source; 相克 control = your discipline). Results render a radar of your five-element profile plus a personalized primary/secondary blend.

Developed from the **Five Elements** section of the [psyverse.fun](https://psyverse.fun) manifesto.
